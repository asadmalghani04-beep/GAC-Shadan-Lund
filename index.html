Heritage of Higher Learning

A Beacon of Collegiate Dignity in Dera Ghazi Khan

Founded in 2021 under the visionary roadmap of Punjab Higher Education Department, Govt. Associate College Shadan Lund has stood as an academic fortress along the historic Indus Highway corridor. We bridge the educational gap for thousands of energetic young men and women from rural and suburban belts of South Punjab.

Our college blends traditional values of personal integrity with contemporary scientific rigor. Fully equipped with cutting-edge science labs, high-speed IT workstations, vast botanical gardens, and a rich 25,000+ volume collegiate repository, we prepare students for direct admissions into top national universities and leadership roles across civil administration and commerce.

Principal’s Anti-Smog Policy Dialogue & Health Briefing

Conducted in the college central library, the seminar brought together faculty members, department heads, and intermediate scholars to establish institutional protocols against seasonal smog. The session highlighted strategic precautions to minimize student health risks during peak hazardous Air Quality Index (AQI) periods.

task_alt Core Seminar Directives

  • AQI Monitoring: Scheduling indoor assemblies and physical education pauses on red-alert smog days.
  • Student Hygiene Defense: Compulsory mask protocol, regular water hydration, and eye protection routines.
  • Zero Campus Emissions: Full ban on open refuse disposal and motor idling inside college grounds.
Venue: Central Library Conference Table

Student Preventive Workshop & Legal Stubble Burning Directives

Dedicated lecture conducted for intermediate and associate degree scholars focusing on the statutory provisions of the Punjab Local Government Act 2022. The session instructed students on the legal prohibitions and criminal liabilities (FIRs and fines) against burning crops, leaves, and plastics.

campaign Student Ambassador Responsibilities

  • Legal Consciousness: Educating agrarian families against stubble combustion under penalty of law.
  • Personal Safety Practice: Wearing masks, washing eyes frequently, and limiting direct contact with smog.
  • Afforestation Commitment: Active participation in tree-planting campaigns across the college perimeter.
Venue: Senior Lecture Hall, Academic Block

Student Code of Conduct

Discipline, Attendance & Collegiate Rules

Govt. Associate College Shadan Lund upholds the highest standards of student discipline, decorum, and academic integrity.

1. Mandatory 75% Attendance: BISE D.G. Khan examination forms are conditional upon maintaining at least 75% classroom and laboratory attendance.

2. Prescribed College Uniform: Students must attend campus in the approved college uniform with institutional student ID card visible.

3. Campus Civility & Cleanliness: Preserving campus lawns, trees, library silence, and classroom apparatus is a shared responsibility.
